Section 1

Before You Start

Before logging into Directus, gather everything listed below. Trying to add a product without these assets leads to incomplete listings and poor SEO.

Assets to Collect

  • Product name — exact model name from the manufacturer (e.g. Hikvision DS-2CD2121G1-I 2MP WDR Fixed Dome Network Camera)
  • Brand name — Hikvision / Dahua / CP Plus / Essl / Matrix / Honeywell etc.
  • SKU / Model number — from the manufacturer datasheet
  • Category — CCTV Cameras / NVR-DVR / Access Control / Networking / Solar CCTV / Biometric
  • MRP / selling price — in INR, inclusive of GST
  • Sale price (if applicable) — must be lower than MRP
  • Stock quantity — how many units are physically in stock
  • Product images — minimum 3 photos (see image rules below)
  • Short description — 1–2 sentences summarising the product
  • Full description — detailed HTML content (see Section 6)
  • Technical specifications — from datasheet (resolution, lens, IR range, etc.)
  • Key features — bullet list of selling points
  • Weight & dimensions — for shipping calculation
  • Tags — relevant search keywords

Image Preparation Rules

📐 Dimensions
Minimum 800×600px. Recommended 1200×900px. Aspect ratio 4:3 for consistency across the grid.
📁 File Format & Size
Use JPG for photos (quality 85). Use PNG only for white-background product cutouts. Max 500 KB per image.
🖼️ What to photograph
Image 1: Front-facing on white/grey background (this becomes the thumbnail).
Image 2: Side or 3/4 angle view.
Image 3: Rear panel / ports / label.
✍️ File Naming
Before uploading, rename files descriptively:
hikvision-ds-2cd2121g1-dome-camera-front.jpg
This helps Google Image Search index the product.
🚫 Never do this with images
  • Do NOT upload images with watermarks or logos from other sites
  • Do NOT upload screenshots — always use real product photos or official press images
  • Do NOT name files IMG_20240312.jpg — use descriptive names
  • Do NOT upload images larger than 2 MB

Section 2

Step-by-Step: Adding a Product

1
Log in to Directus
Go to https://api.infysmart.com/admin. Use your team credentials. You will land on the Collections dashboard.
2
Open the Products collection
In the left sidebar, click Content → Products. You will see a list of all existing products.
3
Click the + button to create a new product
Top-right corner of the Products list. A blank form opens with all fields.
4
Fill fields in order — top to bottom
Follow Sections 3, 4, 5 and 6 of this guide. Do not skip any required field. Fill SEO fields (Section 4) last after the core content is done.
5
Set Status to "Published" only when fully ready
Keep status as Draft while filling fields. Only change to Published after completing the Section 8 checklist. A published product is immediately live and indexed by Google.
6
Save and verify on the live site
After publishing, visit https://infysmart.com/shop/[your-slug] and confirm the product page looks correct before sharing the link.

Section 3

Core Product Fields

name
Required SEO Impact: High
Product Name
The full, official name of the product. This appears as the H1 heading on the product page and is used in the Google Product schema. Use the exact manufacturer model name — do not abbreviate.
✅ Good: Hikvision DS-2CD2121G1-I 2MP WDR Fixed Dome Network Camera
❌ Bad: Hikvision Dome Cam 2MP
SEO Tip: Include the brand, model number, resolution (e.g. 2MP / 4MP) and camera type (dome / bullet / PTZ). These are the exact terms buyers search for.
slug
Required SEO Impact: High
URL Slug
The URL-friendly version of the product name. This becomes part of the permanent product URL: infysmart.com/shop/[slug]. Directus auto-generates this from the name — but always check and clean it up manually.
✅ Good: hikvision-2mp-wdr-fixed-dome-camera-ds-2cd2121g1
❌ Bad: hikvision-ds-2cd2121g1-i-2mp-wdr-fixed-dome-network-camera-1 (too long / has number suffix)
SEO Rule: Slug must be: all lowercase, hyphens only (no underscores or spaces), under 60 characters, include the brand + model + key term. Once a product is published, never change the slug — it breaks existing Google rankings and links.
sku
Required Used in Product Schema
SKU / Model Number
Stock Keeping Unit — the unique identifier for this product. Use the manufacturer's official model number exactly as printed on the box/datasheet. This appears in the Google Product structured data (schema.org) which helps Google Shopping.
✅ Good: DS-2CD2121G1-I  |  DH-IPC-HFW2849S-S-IL  |  CP-UNC-TC21L3C-MD
price
Required
Base Price (₹, incl. GST)
The regular selling price in Indian Rupees, inclusive of 18% GST. Enter as a number only — no ₹ symbol. Example: 2499
Note: The website automatically separates the base price and GST for display. Always enter the final price the customer pays.
sale_price
Optional
Sale / Discounted Price (₹)
Leave blank unless the product is on sale. If filled, it must be lower than the regular price. The discount percentage is calculated and displayed automatically.
stock_quantity
Required
Stock Quantity
Number of units currently in stock. Enter 0 if out of stock. Affects the "In Stock" / "Out of Stock" display and the Google Shopping schema.
track_inventory
Required
Track Inventory
Toggle ON for all physical products. When ON, the site shows "Only X left" warnings when stock drops below 5 units. Toggle OFF only for made-to-order or unlimited items.

Content & Taxonomy Fields

short_description
Required SEO Fallback
Short Description (1–3 sentences)
A concise summary shown on product cards and used as the Google meta description if you don't fill in meta_description. Must be 120–160 characters.
✅ Good: Hikvision 2MP WDR Fixed Dome Camera with H.265+ compression, 30m IR range, and IP67 weatherproof rating. Ideal for indoor and outdoor use in offices, factories and homes.

❌ Bad: Good camera for security.
SEO Tip: Include: brand, resolution, IR distance, weather rating (IP67/IP66), and one use-case (office / home / factory). This text also appears as the Google search result snippet when meta_description is blank.
description
Recommended SEO Impact: Very High Used in Product Schema
Full Description (Rich Text / HTML)
The detailed product page content. This is the most important SEO field after the title. Google reads this to understand what the product is and when to show it in search results. Minimum 150 words. Ideal: 250–400 words.
✅ Recommended structure:
Para 1: What the product is and who it's for.
Para 2: Key technical highlights (resolution, night vision, connectivity).
Para 3: Use cases (home, office, factory, outdoor).
Para 4: Why buy from Infysmart (authorized dealer, warranty, support).
SEO Rule: Naturally include search phrases like "buy [brand] [model] online", "[resolution] CCTV camera price", "indoor/outdoor [type] camera". Do NOT keyword stuff — write for the human reader first.
category
Required
Product Category
Select one from the dropdown. Categories: CCTV Cameras, NVR/DVR, Access Control, Networking, Solar CCTV, Biometric. This drives the shop filter and breadcrumb navigation.
brand
Required
Brand
Select from dropdown: Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, Honeywell, Essl, Matrix. This appears in the Google Product schema as the brand entity, which helps brand-specific searches.
tags
Optional Keyword Fallback
Tags (search keywords array)
An array of keywords associated with this product. These are used as the SEO keyword fallback if meta_keywords is empty. Add one tag per entry. Use terms buyers actually search for.
Example tags for a Hikvision dome camera:
hikvision dome camera · 2mp cctv camera · indoor dome camera · ip67 weatherproof camera · hikvision price india · cctv camera for office
weight
Optional
Weight (kg)
Product weight in kilograms. Example: 0.35. Used in shipping calculation and the Product schema's weight property.
dimensions
Optional
Dimensions (L × W × H in mm)
Product box dimensions in millimetres. Fill when available from the datasheet. Helps with accurate shipping quotes.
is_featured
Optional
Featured Product
Toggle ON for up to 8 products you want displayed on the homepage "Featured Products" section. Featured products get significantly more visibility and traffic. Only mark your best-selling or highest-margin products as featured.

Section 4 — Most Important

SEO Fields — Detailed Guide

These 4 fields directly control what Google shows when someone searches for your product. They also control how your product looks when shared on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.

meta_title
Google Headline Optional (has fallback)
SEO Page Title — The Blue Link on Google
This is the first thing someone sees on a Google search results page. It directly affects your click-through rate (CTR) — a better title means more clicks even at the same rank position.
SEO ImpactCritical
FALLBACK (auto-generated when left blank):
[Product Name] — Buy Online | Infysmart

✅ Better (write this yourself):
Buy Hikvision 2MP Dome Camera DS-2CD2121G1 — ₹2,499 | Infysmart

✅ Also Good:
Hikvision 2MP WDR Dome Camera — IP67, 30m IR, H.265+ | Infysmart Shop
Rules:
· Maximum 60 characters (Google truncates anything longer with "…")
· Always end with "| Infysmart" or "| Infysmart Shop"
· Include: Brand + Resolution/Model + 1 key feature + price (optional)
· Do NOT repeat the same word more than twice
· Do NOT use ALL CAPS
meta_description
Google Snippet Optional (has fallback)
Meta Description — The Grey Text Under the Google Title
The 1–2 line summary shown under the blue link in Google results. Doesn't directly affect ranking but hugely affects whether someone clicks your result. Fallback: uses short_description if left blank.
Click-Through ImpactHigh
✅ Good example:
Buy Hikvision 2MP WDR Fixed Dome Camera at ₹2,499. IP67 waterproof, 30m IR night vision, H.265+ compression. Authorized dealer. 1-year warranty. Free shipping above ₹5,000.

❌ Bad example:
Hikvision dome camera available. Buy now.
Rules:
· Maximum 160 characters
· Include: price (₹X,XXX), 2–3 specs, "authorized dealer" or "1-year warranty", call to action
· Use Indian Rupee symbol ₹ — it stands out in search results
· Write as if convincing someone to click in 2 seconds

Keywords & Social Image

meta_keywords
New Field Search Signals Optional (has fallback)
SEO Keywords — Comma-Separated Search Terms
A comma-separated list of keywords that describe this product. These are embedded in the page HTML and in the Product structured data (schema.org). Fallback: uses the tags field if left blank.
Schema/AI Search ImpactMedium-High
✅ Good example (for a Hikvision 2MP dome camera):
hikvision dome camera, 2mp cctv camera, hikvision ds-2cd2121g1 price, buy hikvision camera online, indoor cctv camera, IP67 dome camera, hikvision camera price india, cctv camera for office

How to find the right keywords

Brand + Model hikvision ds-2cd2121g1 price
Brand + Type hikvision dome camera india
Resolution + Type 2mp dome camera price
Use Case cctv camera for office
Buy Intent buy hikvision camera online
Location cctv camera price chennai
Rules:
· Add 6–12 keywords per product
· Separate each keyword with a comma and a space
· Include both short (2-word) and long (4-word) keyword phrases
· Do NOT repeat keywords you already have in the meta_title
· Think: "What would a buyer type into Google to find this product?"
og_image
New Field Optional (has fallback)
Social Share Image (OG Image)
The image that appears when this product link is shared on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, or LinkedIn. Fallback order: og_image → thumbnail → site default image. You only need to upload this if you want a custom banner/promotional image for social sharing.
✅ When to use

For sale/discount banners, promotional campaigns, or when the product thumbnail doesn't look great as a landscape image.

📐 Required Size

1200 × 630 pixels (landscape). JPG format. Include product photo + price + brand logo for best engagement.

For most products, leave this blank. The product thumbnail is automatically cropped to 1200×630 and used as the OG image. Only upload a custom OG image for featured or promotional products.

Images — Upload Rules

thumbnail
Required SEO Impact: High
Primary / Thumbnail Image
The main product image. Shown on product cards in the shop grid, in the image gallery, in Google Search image results, in cart, and as the social share image fallback. This is the single most visible image for the product.
Requirements: JPG · Min 800×600px · Recommended 1200×900px · Aspect ratio 4:3 · White or light grey background · Clear product view (front-facing) · Under 500 KB
SEO Tip: Rename the file before uploading: hikvision-2mp-dome-camera-ds2cd2121.jpg Google indexes the filename as a signal for image search. Generic names like IMG_001.jpg get no benefit.
images
Recommended Schema: Up to 5 images
Gallery Images (Multiple)
Additional product images shown in the page image gallery. The product page shows an interactive gallery. Google's Product schema includes up to 5 images — more images = richer Google Shopping listing.
Image #What to showAlt Text to enter
Image 1Front view (matches thumbnail)Hikvision 2MP Dome Camera — Front View
Image 2Side / 3/4 angle viewHikvision DS-2CD2121G1 — Side View
Image 3Rear panel / cable portsHikvision Dome Camera — Rear Ports & Cable
Image 4Installed / in-use photoHikvision Dome Camera installed in office ceiling
Image 5Box / packaging + accessoriesHikvision DS-2CD2121G1 box contents
Critical — Always fill Alt Text for every image. Alt text is read by: (1) Google Image Search — to understand and rank your image (2) Screen readers — for accessibility compliance (3) Google Product schema — for richer search results Format: [Brand] [Model] [Camera Type] — [Angle/Context]
📸 Image Alt Text Formula

[Brand] [Resolution] [Camera Type] [Model] — [View/Context]

Examples:

  • Hikvision 2MP Fixed Dome Network Camera DS-2CD2121G1 — Front View
  • Dahua 4MP Bullet Camera DH-IPC-HFW2849S — Outdoor Installed
  • CP Plus 8 Channel DVR CPUAT0801H2M1 — Rear Panel Ports

features
Recommended SEO Impact: Medium
Key Features (Bullet list)
A list of the product's main selling points shown as bullet points on the product page. Each entry should be a complete, benefit-focused sentence. Add 5–8 features per product.
✅ Good feature entries:
· 2MP Full HD resolution for crisp, clear surveillance footage
· 30m infrared night vision for 24/7 monitoring in complete darkness
· H.265+ compression reduces storage by up to 75% vs. standard H.264
· IP67 weatherproof rating for reliable outdoor installation
· PoE support (IEEE 802.3af) — single cable for power and data
· Works with Hikvision NVR and iVMS-4200 monitoring software

❌ Bad feature entries:
· Good quality · Nice camera · HD

Section 7

SEO Writing Rules for Product Content

Rule 1: Write the Full Description for Both Humans AND Google

Google's algorithm reads your product description the same way a human would. It looks for natural language that answers the question: "Is this the product the searcher is looking for?"

Write as if you're explaining the product to a buyer who can't see it in person. Be specific about what it does, who it's for, and why it's better than generic alternatives.

Rule 2: The Keyword Placement Priority

LocationPriorityHow to use keywords
Product Name (H1)HighestBrand + Model + Resolution + Type. Be exact.
meta_titleHighestPrimary keyword + price or key feature
meta_descriptionHighSecondary keywords + price + trust signals
Short DescriptionHigh2–3 natural keyword phrases in 1–2 sentences
Full Description Para 1MediumState product name + primary keyword naturally
Specifications tableMediumTechnical keywords appear naturally here
Feature bulletsGoodLong-tail keywords hidden naturally in benefits
Image alt textGoodBrand + model + type + angle
meta_keywords / tagsSignalsAll additional keyword variants

Rule 3: Never Keyword Stuff

❌ Keyword Stuffing — BAD

"Buy Hikvision CCTV camera Hikvision price Hikvision dome camera CCTV camera price CCTV camera India buy CCTV camera online…"

Google penalises this. Your page will rank lower.

✅ Natural Language — GOOD

"The Hikvision DS-2CD2121G1 is a 2MP Full HD dome camera designed for indoor and outdoor surveillance. With H.265+ compression and 30m IR range, it's ideal for offices and small factories."

Rule 4: Use Indian Context

🇮🇳 India-specific SEO signals that boost local rankings
  • Always include the ₹ symbol with price in descriptions: "available at ₹2,499"
  • Mention Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Chennai, Hosur where relevant for local SEO
  • Include "GST invoice", "authorized dealer", "pan-India delivery" — these are high-trust phrases Indian buyers search for
  • Mention "1-year warranty" and "Infysmart installation available" to differentiate from grey-market sellers

Section 8 — Final Step

QA Checklist — Before You Hit Publish

Run through every item below before changing the product status to Published. A product with even one missing item ranks lower and converts worse.

✅ Identity & Pricing

  • Product name includes: Brand + Model Number + Resolution/Spec + Camera Type
  • Slug is clean, lowercase, under 60 chars, with hyphens only
  • SKU matches the official manufacturer model number exactly
  • Price is entered in INR inclusive of GST (not ex-GST)
  • If a sale price exists, it is lower than the regular price
  • Stock quantity is correct and track_inventory is toggled ON
  • Category and Brand are selected from the dropdown

✅ Content

  • Short description is 120–160 characters and includes brand, resolution, key spec
  • Full description is at least 150 words, structured in paragraphs
  • Full description naturally contains primary keywords (not stuffed)
  • At least 5 specification rows are filled from the datasheet
  • At least 5 feature bullet points are filled (complete benefit sentences)
  • Tags array has at least 5 relevant keyword entries

✅ Images

  • Thumbnail image uploaded — min 800×600px, JPG, on white/grey background
  • At least 2 additional gallery images uploaded (3+ recommended)
  • Every gallery image has a descriptive Alt Text filled in
  • No watermarks, no screenshots, no blurry images

✅ SEO Fields

  • meta_title written — under 60 characters, includes brand + model + "| Infysmart"
  • meta_description written — under 160 chars, includes price (₹) + trust signal
  • meta_keywords filled — 6–12 comma-separated keyword phrases
  • og_image uploaded if this is a featured/promotional product (optional otherwise)

✅ Final Checks

  • Status changed to "Published" only after all above items are complete
  • Visited the live URL: infysmart.com/shop/[slug] and confirmed correct display
  • Checked that the product thumbnail and price appear correctly in the shop grid
  • If featured (is_featured = ON): confirmed it appears on the homepage
🚀 Once Published — What Happens Automatically
  • The product page is added to the sitemap within 60 seconds (Google crawls sitemaps regularly)
  • The product schema (JSON-LD) is embedded: tells Google the price, brand, availability and shipping details
  • The OG/Twitter meta tags are set: sharing the URL on WhatsApp/social shows the correct image and title
  • Google typically indexes a new product within 1–7 days of the sitemap being updated
